# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Write issues and pull requests in Portuguese or English.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions or spamming.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/NicolasPortie) or use the private **Report a vulnerability** form in the Security tab if it involves sensitive information. Reports are handled confidentially.
